3. Connect your domain and test HTTPS.
4. Verify the exact office pin in Google Maps and correct it if needed.
5. Set up/claim the Google Business Profile for Sai Darshan Transport and keep the name, phone, address and website consistent.
6. Submit sitemap.xml after adding it; for this single page, sitemap.xml should contain the final homepage URL.
7. Add Search Console and request indexing after launch.

Important:
